In summer of 2026, Sarah Hamid (board member of <a href="https://lucyparsonslabs.com/" target="_blank">Lucy Parsons Labs</a>) and worker at the Electronic Frontier Foundation did a presentation with me at DEFCON 34 on ALPRs. Our talk covers the many undemocratic ways that tech companies are subverting popular opinion to maximize their profits and deploy surveillance across the country. It also went into the research that's unfolded across the US to track the proliferation of companies like Flock. Sarah and I also spent quite a bit of time talking about how the many, many attempts to regulate this tech has been nothing short of an abysmal failure.
